About the notes

The built-in melody is a sketch of the A section written from memory. The first two bars should be right. Later bars are only close to what Bach wrote, so don't learn the piece from it.

To see Bach's actual notes, load a MIDI file of the Badinerie: drop it onto the lane or use Load MIDI. Free files and scores: IMSLP, flutetunes.com.

The piece

The seventh and last movement of the suite, for solo flute with strings and continuo. It is in 2/4 and in binary form: an A section of 16 bars that ends in F♯ minor, then a B section of 24 bars that returns to B minor. Each half is repeated.
