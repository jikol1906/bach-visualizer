About the notes

All five parts come from John Hamm's General MIDI sequence of the Orchestral Suites (1995): flute, violin I, violin II, viola and cello. The cello plays the continuo bass. All repeats are played, and Hamm added a few ornaments of his own.

You can load any other MIDI file with Load MIDI or by dropping it onto the lane. Each track becomes a voice. More scores: IMSLP.

The piece

The seventh and last movement of the suite, for solo flute with strings and continuo. It is in 2/4 and in binary form: an A section of 16 bars that ends in F♯ minor, then a B section of 24 bars that returns to B minor. Each half is repeated, and it opens with a quarter-note pickup.
