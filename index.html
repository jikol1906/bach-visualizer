Playing it

Hover over any note to hear it. Click a voice above to hide and mute it, or its ◎ to put it in the spotlight (click several ◎ to spotlight several voices): the other voices keep playing but step back. Drag or scroll the lane sideways to move through the piece. Space plays and pauses, ← → jump a bar (or use −1 bar and −5 bars), Home goes back to the start. Piano and Guitar show a keyboard and two guitar necks under the lane. The keyboard lights up each note as it sounds, in its voice's colour, and you can press its keys (or slide along them) to play along. The two guitar necks below it do the same: one in standard tuning, one tuned lower for the bass line. Each note goes on one string, near where that voice's hand already is, and you can press any fret to play it.

To hear the harmony, pause and turn on Hold chord. Every note under the line keeps sounding until the line leaves it. Drag the line itself, or drag the score under it. ← → then step from one note to the next, and Shift + ← → jump a bar. Arpeggio (or A) switches on a running arpeggio of the held chord, up and down, that follows the line until you switch it off. Arp sets its speed in notes per second.

To sing along, pick a voice, turn on Sing and allow the microphone. The music starts a bar before that voice's next note and plays on without it, in time. Sing its notes, in any octave: your pitch is drawn as a line over the score, and every note you hit in time gets a gold bar underneath. Miss one and the music stops on it, holds the harmony and plays the note for you; it carries on as soon as you sing it (Space skips it). Notes too quick to sing at the current speed never stop the music, so slow down with Speed to be asked for more of them. Guide plays your voice softly along with you. Use headphones, or the microphone will hear the speakers. H or a tap on the tag plays the next note.

About the notes

All seven movements come from John Hamm's General MIDI sequence of the Orchestral Suites (1995), with five parts each: flute, violin I, violin II, viola and cello. The cello plays the continuo bass. All repeats are played, the tempo follows Hamm's own changes and ritardandos, and he added a few ornaments of his own.

You can load any other MIDI file with Load MIDI or by dropping it onto the lane. Each track becomes a voice. More scores: IMSLP.
